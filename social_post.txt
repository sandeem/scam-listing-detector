I've been apartment hunting, and the same listing keeps showing up in different clothes: the landlord is overseas, the rent is half of what the neighbourhood goes for, and the deposit is due before you ever see the door. 😤

After nearly falling for one, I stopped trying to spot them by eye and built something instead.

Scam Listing Detector is a Chrome extension that labels rental listings while you scroll: 🟢 Low risk, 🟡 Suspicious, 🔴 Likely scam. Hover any badge and it tells you exactly why.

🔎 Jev answers eleven small yes/no questions about each listing and hands back probabilities, so the reading is the model's and the weighing is ours.

📊 Glasser brings the outside evidence: web scam reports, an address lookup, a reverse image search on the photos, and public property records.

The extension never touches an API key. A small local relay holds them, so the browser only ever sees a verdict.

Built with AdaL & Glasser at the What Would Jev Do? hackathon.

A verdict is a risk signal, not proof. Don't accuse anyone.
